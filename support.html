Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access teaching resources? ▼
You can browse and access teaching resources by visiting our Resources page. Use the search bar to find specific materials or browse by category. Most resources are available for free, while some premium content requires a subscription.
Can I share resources with other teachers? ▼
Yes! You can share resources with colleagues through the sharing options available on each resource page. Simply click the share button and choose your preferred sharing method (email, link, or social media).
How do I create an account? ▼
Creating an account is easy! Click the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner, fill in your details, and verify your email address. Once verified, you'll have full access to save resources, track your progress, and connect with other educators.
What file formats are available for download? ▼
Resources are available in various formats including PDF, DOCX, PPTX, and interactive formats. The available formats are listed on each resource page. Most resources can be downloaded and edited to suit your classroom needs.
How can I contribute my own teaching materials? ▼
We welcome contributions from educators! Click on "Contribute" in your account dashboard to upload your materials. All submissions are reviewed by our team before being published to ensure quality and alignment with educational standards.
What are your support hours? ▼
Our AI chat assistant is available 24/7. Email support is monitored Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM EST. Phone support is available Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM EST. We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours.
